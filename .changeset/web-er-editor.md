Add the ER visual editor: entity tables with typed attributes, PK/FK/UK key badges and comments; add, edit, reorder and remove attributes in the inspector; relationships with crow's-foot ends, identifying or non-identifying lines and labels (edited in place or in the inspector), reverse; aliases, groups, direction and auto layout; connect by dragging a handle (or drop on empty canvas for a related entity), inline renaming, delete and keyboard nudges. Every edit is one labelled undo step, and the selection is shared with Split and the rest of the app.
