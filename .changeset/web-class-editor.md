Add the UML class visual editor: classes, interfaces, abstract classes and enums with stereotypes and generics, attributes and methods (visibility, static, abstract) edited in the inspector or in place, reordered and removed, every relation kind with labels and multiplicities, lollipop interfaces, notes, namespaces (group, rename, ungroup, drag the frame), direction, auto layout, duplicate (⌘D), keyboard nudges and connecting by dragging a handle. Every edit is one labelled undo step, and the selection is shared with the rest of the app by class name.
