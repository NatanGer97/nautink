Import and back up: drop `.mmd`, `.mermaid` or `.md` files (one diagram per mermaid block) anywhere on the window or pick them with "Open file…" (⌘K, Diagrams panel footer); a dropped backup is restored. Settings "Back up all" downloads every diagram, version and position as JSON, and "Restore backup" merges a Nautink or v1 backup.
